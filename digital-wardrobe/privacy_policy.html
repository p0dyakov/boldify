2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address, display name, authentication identifiers, and profile information received from Google or Apple when you choose those sign-in options.
  • Wardrobe content: photos of clothing you upload, outfit layouts, folders, preferences, and related metadata you create in the app.
  • Profile and styling preferences: optional information you provide during onboarding or in settings, such as gender and age group, used to personalize outfit suggestions.
  • Calendar context: if you connect a calendar, we read event titles, times, locations, and all-day flags from the selected calendar to improve outfit recommendations. We do not collect attendee lists or message content from calendar events.
  • Support communications: messages and information you send through in-app support or email.
  • Promo or feedback information: information you submit when redeeming promotions or sending feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app data: device model, operating system, app version, language, identifiers, IP address, and diagnostic logs.
  • Usage data: feature interactions, session activity, crash reports, and performance events.
  • Location data: approximate location only if you grant permission, used for weather context and related features.
  • Subscription metadata: product identifiers, subscription status, and transaction IDs received from Apple or Google to manage premium access. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Push notification tokens: if you enable notifications.

Information generated by the Service

  • AI-generated outputs: clothing cutouts, tags, outfit suggestions, styling comments, and other generated metadata created from your uploaded content or prompts.

Permissions you may grant

Depending on how you use the Service, the app may request access to your camera, photo library, storage, calendar, microphone (for optional support chat voice messages), location, and notifications. You can change these permissions in your device settings.

Sensitive information

We do not ask you to provide sensitive categories of personal data such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, or biometric identifiers for identification purposes.
