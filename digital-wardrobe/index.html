Clovet

Dress smarter with AI-powered outfit planning

Your closet, organized and styled

Clovet turns photos of your clothes into a smart wardrobe you can style every day. Snap your pieces, build outfits on a visual canvas, and get AI suggestions tailored to your calendar, weather, and plans.

Whether you are getting dressed for right now or planning a trip, Clovet helps you wear more of what you own with less daily friction.

Key Features

AI Wardrobe Builder

Photograph your clothes and let AI extract cutouts, categories, colors, and metadata automatically.

Outfit Canvas

Drag and layer items on a visual canvas to compose looks, save outfits, and organize them into folders.

For Now

Get wear-now outfit recommendations based on your wardrobe, local weather, and upcoming calendar events.

Smart Folders

Describe your plans and let AI generate curated outfit capsules for trips, events, or weekly rotations.

Refine with AI

Ask for styling tweaks on any outfit and receive concrete add, replace, or remove suggestions from your wardrobe.

Wear Tracking

Log what you wear, see activity insights per item, and make better use of pieces you already own.
